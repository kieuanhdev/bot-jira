Execute the AutoImprove protocol defined by the `auto-improve` agent.

Use `autoimprove/goal.md` as the objective. Process one improvement only. Audit
without editing, reject low-value findings, then show the selected issue, scope,
risk, and `fast` or `full` validation plan. Ask the developer for explicit approval
before baseline or source edits. After approval, capture or safely reuse a matching
baseline, implement, independently review, validate with the same approved scope,
perform only evidence-led bounded repairs, and write a timestamped report. Stop
safely with no source edit when approval is declined or prerequisites are blocked.
