risk, and `fast` or `full` validation plan. Use concise English for all internal
work and subagent communication. Show the proposal and ask the developer for
explicit approval in Vietnamese before baseline or source edits. After approval,
capture or safely reuse a matching
baseline, implement, independently review, validate with the same approved scope,
perform only evidence-led bounded repairs, and write a timestamped report. Stop
safely with no source edit when approval is declined or prerequisites are blocked.
Write the report and final developer-facing response in Vietnamese.
